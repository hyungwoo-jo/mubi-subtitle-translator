Subtitle Translator for MUBI privacy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Subtitle Translator for MUBI is an independent subtitle translator, tested with MUBI. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by MUBI.

Experimental browser-only ChatGPT mode

Official ChatGPT OAuth runs in a separate sign-in window. The extension validates the returned identity and plan permission, then sends subtitle requests directly to the public OpenAI Responses API. Tokens stay only in the connection tab’s memory; closing it clears local credentials. Basic account labels, issued client IDs and an opaque host identifier are stored locally for later sign-in. No credential is placed in diagnostics or browser storage. A connection tab must remain open while using this prototype.

What is processed

On sites you enable, the extension reads subtitle text, timing data and nearby dialogue. In browser-only ChatGPT mode, it sends dialogue and translation preferences directly to OpenAI. Optional legacy modes send them to the local helper. With Codex selected, the helper sends them to OpenAI through your own Codex sign-in. With Ollama selected, the helper connects to 127.0.0.1:11434 on this PC. Local processing requires a locally running model; Ollama cloud models or your own server configuration may forward data.

What stays on your device

Preferences, translated dialogue and numerical usage totals are stored in browser local storage. Diagnostic files contain hostnames, element structure, counts and response metadata; they exclude dialogue text. No project-operated analytics, advertising or translation relay server is used.

Your choices

Translation requires your confirmation and permission for the selected site. Disable a site in the popup, clear translations in Settings, or remove the extension to delete its browser storage. Local helper diagnostic files remain on the PC until you remove them.

Accounts and providers

The extension does not ask for your ChatGPT password, read authentication cookies or copy Codex login tokens. Codex handles its own authentication. Your provider's terms, retention rules, charges and account limits apply.

Use and support

Use only with content and services where you have the necessary rights and permission. Site terms may restrict automated access and translation. Open an issue in the Subtitle Translator for MUBI repository for questions; do not attach private subtitles, account credentials or signed media URLs.
