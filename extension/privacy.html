CueBraid privacy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

CueBraid is an independent subtitle translator, tested with MUBI. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by MUBI.

What is processed

On sites you enable, the extension reads subtitle text, timing data and nearby dialogue. It sends the dialogue and your translation preferences to the local helper. With Codex selected, the helper sends them to OpenAI through your own Codex sign-in. With Ollama selected, the helper connects to 127.0.0.1:11434 on this PC. Local processing requires a locally running model; Ollama cloud models or your own server configuration may forward data.

What stays on your device

Preferences, translated dialogue and numerical usage totals are stored in browser local storage. Diagnostic files contain hostnames, element structure, counts and response metadata; they exclude dialogue text. No project-operated analytics, advertising or translation relay server is used.

Your choices

Translation requires your confirmation and permission for the selected site. Disable a site in the popup, clear translations in Settings, or remove the extension to delete its browser storage. Local helper diagnostic files remain on the PC until you remove them.

Accounts and providers

The extension does not ask for your ChatGPT password, read authentication cookies or copy Codex login tokens. Codex handles its own authentication. Your provider's terms, retention rules, charges and account limits apply.

Use and support

Use only with content and services where you have the necessary rights and permission. Site terms may restrict automated access and translation. Open an issue in the CueBraid repository for questions; do not attach private subtitles, account credentials or signed media URLs.
